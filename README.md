# The Worst Pizza Topping

One funny line, 114 characters, for [The Worst Pizza Topping](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-the-worst-pizza-topping-062c6893) bounty on make.fun:

> Try our durian pizza: the topping smells so bad the delivery driver leaves it on the curb and texts you good luck.

The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

- **Terrible topping, named:** durian on pizza — a real topping famous for smelling so bad it is banned from hotels and airports.
- **A real joke with a twist:** the pitch turns on the delivery driver abandoning the pizza on the curb and texting "good luck" — the smell defeats even the person paid to deliver it.
- **Vivid and specific:** curb, driver, text — concrete images, no vague insult.
- **One line, clean pitch:** 114 characters, a single sentence that reads like something a shop would actually say to sell the idea.
